Check a website

Get a plain-language report on a site's speed, phone layout, Google search, accessibility and trust, ready to send to the owner.

An audit usually takes 1 to 3 minutes. More pages and Lighthouse runs take longer.

What the report covers

Speed
How fast the site loads on a phone, measured by Google's own test.
Phones
Whether pages fit the screen, numbers are tap-to-call and buttons are easy to tap.
Google search
Titles, descriptions, headings, sitemap, share previews and local business details.
Accessibility
Image descriptions, readable contrast, form labels and page language.
Trust & contact
Broken links and images, HTTPS, ways to get in touch and an up-to-date footer.
