Fix firstPhones

1 page isn't set up for phones

Without a viewport tag, phones show a shrunken desktop page that visitors have to pinch and zoom to read.

How to fix Add <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1"> to the <head> of every page.

Where /about.html

Fix firstPhones

1 page is wider than a phone screen

Depending on the phone, the page wobbles sideways as visitors scroll, opens zoomed out with tiny text, or cuts off whatever sticks out past the right edge. Each one looks broken.

How to fix Let the wide element fit: max-width: 100% on images, tables and embeds, allow long words, emails and links to wrap, and check which header or menu shows at phone widths.

Fix soonPhones

The phone number isn't tap-to-call

On a phone, people expect to tap a number to call it. Making them copy it by hand loses calls.

How to fix Wrap the number in a call link, e.g. <a href="tel:+15551234567">(555) 123-4567</a>.

Fix soonGoogle search

2 pages share the same title

Each page's title is its headline in Google. Identical titles make pages compete with each other, and Google may show the wrong one.

How to fix Write a different title for each page that says what that page is about.

Where / /about.html

  • "Home" on 2 pages

Fix soonGoogle search

1 page has no search description

The description is the text under your title in Google. Without one, Google picks a random snippet from the page, which often reads poorly and gets fewer clicks.

How to fix Write a 120–160 character description for each page that says what you offer and where.

Where /about.html

Fix soonGoogle search

Google doesn't get your business details in a form it can read

Structured data (LocalBusiness) gives Google your name, address, phone and hours exactly. It helps local searches and Google Maps match the site to your business.

How to fix Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD to the homepage with your name, address, phone, opening hours and website, matching your Google Business Profile.

Where /

Fix soonAccessibility

1 image has no description (alt text)

Blind visitors hear nothing (or a file name) where the image is, and Google can't tell what the photo shows, so it won't appear in image search.

How to fix Describe each meaningful image in its alt attribute, such as alt="Technician fixing a water heater". Decorative images get alt="".

  • photo.svg
